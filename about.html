ABOUT

I started AOISEIKOU around cars, people, documentation, and the things that happen around them. What it becomes from here isn't something I need to force.

CURRENT DIRECTION LET IT MOVE.
01

I don't need AOISEIKOU to become huge.

Life isn't going to stay the way it was when I started this. I'm older, I'm busier, and I'm going to have my own things to worry about.

So I'm not trying to force AOISEIKOU into becoming some massive organization. I'll do my own thing here. I'll document what I want to document, archive what matters to me, and let everyone else do their thing too.

Sometimes something will happen. Sometimes nothing will. That's fine.

03

WINGS

You don't have to be given a role. You can build one.

This is probably the most important change I've made to how I think about AOISEIKOU.

If I trust someone, I don't need to tell them exactly what they should do. They can build something of their own under AOISEIKOU and take it in whatever direction makes sense to them.

Quinton has his own wing. Cece can have his own wing. Someone else could eventually do the same.

The point isn't to create another hierarchy. It's to give people somewhere to build.

04

REPRESENTATION

AOISEIKOU doesn't have to be in the room to be represented.

People around AOISEIKOU can show up elsewhere, participate in other communities, document things, or simply be themselves while carrying AOISEIKOU with them.

I don't expect to be present at everything. Sometimes someone else will be there instead, and that's completely fine.

At the same time, being associated with AOISEIKOU doesn't make everything someone does an official AOISEIKOU activity.

PRESENCE ISN'T LIMITED TO ONE PERSON.
