The True Beginning

The first ever Aoiseikou Festival

DATE / 18.03.2026 LOCATION / — I forgot

Hi Everybody. On 8/03/2026, Aoiseikou hosted the first Aoiseikou Festival, and it was one hell of an experience!

Going into it, I was expecting a few people. I had a plan in mind — how the meet would run, how things would be organized. Nothing too crazy, just something manageable.
But hey, life is all about unexpected things. As people started joining, the numbers kept climbing. What was supposed to be a small, controlled meet quickly started slipping out of that shape.
And before I could process what was happening, the server was already full with people from different groups like LowCTRL, Saku, Left4Static.

I’m gonna be a critique on my own meet here — I didn’t expect so many people to get in, and hence there wasn’t a proper staff team.
People began breaking formation. Instructions weren’t followed. What was meant to be structured quickly turned messy.
At some point, even things like trucks started showing up — completely out of place for what the meet was supposed to be. It was frustrating to watch at times — but it also showed where things needed to improve.

We did try to bring things back with a drift section later on, but even there, control was hard to maintain. People pushed limits without really understanding them, and it showed.

But it wasn’t all falling apart. As the meet went on, things started finding their rhythm again. The numbers dipped a bit, but the energy didn’t disappear. The drifts were amazing, people did tandems — Overall it was amazing. For e.g., Rde, Itsuki and Zeno, Mia and Cuban. They were absolutely killing it!

EDIT: Continue the article.

There aren’t many photos from that night. Most of it wasn’t captured properly. But maybe that’s what makes it what it was. It wasn’t staged, it wasn’t documented perfectly — it just happened.

Kind of ironic — the beginning of #DocumentingCulture started with a not-so-documented meet.
